Build community with other educational consultants.

Solopreneur? Need a break? Want to build a community while exploring a new culture? Common Ground was designed to be deliberately small and slow with 25 Independent Educational Consultants (IECs) max. Part professional retreat, part vacation: three nights on an organic coffee farm in the Colombian cloud forest, with long meals, good programming, and time to actually enjoy where you are.

The place

A working coffee farm in the cloud-forest hills

Stay in a room in the middle of an organic coffee farm designed around principles of sustainability and woven into its natural surroundings in the rolling, green hills of Cundinamarca, a short drive from Bogotá. The coffee grown here is served in some of the best cafés in the world. You will drink it steps from where it was picked. Plenty of people come as much for the place as for the program, and there is no rule that says a work trip cannot also be a vacation.

Choose between a single (limited capacity, first-come first-serve) or double. Each room is fully independent and includes a bedroom area, private bathroom, and a balcony where you can enjoy spectacular views.

Who it's for

For your first year or your fiftieth

If you're newer to the work

You will leave with structure: how experienced IECs run their practices, price their services, and manage families, plus new colleagues you can call on for help when you need it.

If you're a veteran

You will leave with what is hardest to find at your stage: peers who operate at your level, honest conversations about where the profession is heading, and a few days of genuine rest in a place worth traveling to. Bring a partner, add a few days in Bogotá, and it becomes a vacation too.

Good to know

Questions we expect you have

How do I get there?

Fly into Bogotá's El Dorado International Airport (BOG), which has direct flights from many US, European, and Latin American cities. The farm is a scenic drive from the city, and we will coordinate arrival logistics with the group.

I've never been to Colombia. Is it beginner-friendly?

Very. You will be with a group the entire time, the venue is accustomed to international guests, and we will send a full travel guide covering everything from packing to pesos before you go.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. The program is in English, and the excursions are guided.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made by December 15, 2026 receive a 50% refund. After December 15, registrations are non-refundable.

Can I stay longer and see Bogotá?

Yes, and we would encourage it. Tell us in your registration form and we will help you arrange sightseeing in the city before or after the retreat.
