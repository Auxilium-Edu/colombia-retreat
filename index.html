The place

A working coffee farm in the cloud-forest hills

Stay in a cabin in the middle of an organic coffee farm designed around principles of sustainability and woven into its natural surroundings in the rolling, green hills of Cundinamarca, a short drive from Bogotá. The coffee grown here is served in some of the best cafés in the world. You will drink it steps from where it was picked.

Choose between a single (limited capacity, first-come first-serve) or double. Each cabin is fully independent and includes a bedroom area, private bathroom, outdoor shower and a balcony where you can enjoy spectacular views.

The program

Two full days of substance, held loosely enough to breathe

Sessions are built for a mixed room, so newer IECs leave with foundations and structure while veterans leave with fresh thinking and peers who operate at their level. The excursions are not filler; they are where the best conversations tend to happen.

Beyond the sessions

Colombia, up close

Every activity is included in the price and entirely optional. Join what sounds fun, skip what doesn’t, and nobody will mind.

Coffee tasting

A guided cupping on one of Colombia's most celebrated specialty farms, led by the people who grow it.

Exotic fruit tour

Lulo, guanábana, granadilla and more. A tasting tour of fruit most of the world has never met.

Latin dancing

Salsa, rumba, or cha-cha in a beginner-friendly lesson, because you cannot come to Colombia and not dance at least once.

Hiking

Trails through coffee trees and cloud forest, with views that make the early alarm worth it.

Mountain biking

The farm keeps bikes and forest trails for anyone who'd rather roll than walk. All levels welcome.

Horseback riding

See the countryside the way it has been seen for generations: from the saddle, at a walking pace.

Good to know

Questions we expect you have

How do I get there?

Fly into Bogotá's El Dorado International Airport (BOG), which has direct flights from many US, European, and Latin American cities. The farm is a scenic drive from the city, and we will coordinate arrival logistics with the group.

I've never been to Colombia. Is it beginner-friendly?

Very. You will be with a group the entire time, the venue is accustomed to international guests, and we will send a full travel guide covering everything from packing to pesos before you go.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. The program is in English, and the excursions are guided.

Can I stay longer and see Bogotá?

Yes, and we would encourage it. Tell us in your application and we will help you arrange sightseeing in the city before or after the retreat.
